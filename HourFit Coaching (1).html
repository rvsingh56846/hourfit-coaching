Questions

What happens after I send the form?

Your coach reads your details and confirms the date and time by email or phone, then shares the video link.

Can I choose a woman or a man coach?

Yes. The coach type is part of the plan you pick. Both cost the same: $10 for 1 hour or $18 for 2 hours.

Which U.S. time zones do you cover?

All of them: Eastern, Central, Mountain, Arizona, Pacific, Alaska and Hawaii. Pick yours in the form and your start time is read in that zone.

Do I need equipment?

No. Tell your coach what you have at home in the notes field and the workout is planned around it.

Can I change my plan before sending?

Yes. Use the plan selector at the top of the form to switch coach or length at any time.

Is the plan safe for me?

Share any injuries or conditions in the notes field. If you have a medical condition, check with your doctor before you start.
